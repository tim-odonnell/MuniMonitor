           TO:            Assessor, Equalization Directors and Interested Parties

           FROM:          Michigan State Tax Commission

           SUBJECT:       Recalculating Taxable Value for Assessment Years Which Follow the
                          Years Changed by an Order of the State Tax Commission or the Michigan
                          Tax Tribunal

           Bulletin No. 18 of 2017 is rescinded.

           The State Tax Commission directs that whenever an Order for a change in Taxable
           Value is received by an Assessor from the State Tax Commission (STC) or the
           Michigan Tax Tribunal (MTT), the Assessor shall recalculate the Taxable Values for the
           year(s) following the year(s) specifically included in the Order. The recalculation for the
           subsequent year(s) shall be based on the Taxable Value ordered by the STC or MTT for
           the most recent year included in the Order.

           The STC establishes the following procedures, which shall be used by Assessors when
           the recalculation of Taxable Value is required following the issuance of an STC Order or
           MTT Order.

                1) Upon receipt of an STC Order or an MTT Order directing a change in Taxable
                   Value, the Assessor shall immediately recalculate the Taxable Value(s) for the
                   year(s) following the most recent year included in the Order.

                2) For a correction resulting from an STC Order, the Assessor shall use Form 6215
                   Assessor Affidavit Regarding the Recalculation of Taxable Value Following a
                   State Tax Commission Order for a Prior Year.

                    Form 6215 shall not be filed to adjust any tax year impacted by an MTT
                    decision or other court decision.

                3) For a correction resulting from an MTT Order, the Assessor shall use Form 3452,
                   Assessor Affidavit Regarding the Recalculation of Taxable Value Following an
                   MTT Order for a Prior Year.

  4) The Assessor shall immediately correct the assessment roll(s) and tax roll(s) for
     the applicable year(s) by entering the recalculated Taxable Value(s) on the
     roll(s). Within 30 days, the Assessor shall file an affidavit with the Equalization
     Department, County Treasurer, and the treasurers of all other affected taxing
     authorities responsible for the assessment figures, rate of taxation, or
     mathematical calculations so all official records may be corrected.

  5) For each tax year in which the tax roll is corrected, a corrected tax bill shall be
     issued by the tax collecting unit in possession of the respective tax roll.
