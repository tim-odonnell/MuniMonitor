 The materials listed below are typical of a Class C house. Variations from these specifications can be found.
 Substitute materials of equivalent price and quality may be used in place of the materials listed below. The pricing
 examples and the adjustments and additions section contain types of variations which normally require
 adjustments. NOTE: The single- family sections, include base interior wall height of 8 feet for each floor. For each
 foot of variation, add to or deduct from that portion of the residence base cost only: 4% for all residence exterior
 masonry walls, including brick and stone veneers; 3% for siding exterior walls. See Page 4 for further information
 and calculations.
