     TO:            Assessors and Equalization Directors

     FROM:          Michigan State Tax Commission

     SUBJECT: Random Week for “Qualified Business”

     Public Act (PA) 96 of 1994 provides that in certain circumstances the “qualified personal
     property” for a “qualified business” (usually a leasing company) may be assessed to the
     user of that personal property rather than to its owner (the leasing company). See State
     Tax Commission Bulletin No. 16 of 1994 for more information.

     PA 96 of 1994 states that one of the requirements of a “qualified business” is that the
     business must have 30 or fewer employees during a week selected at random each year
     by the State Tax Commission.

     The week selected at random by the State Tax Commission for 2025 assessment
     purposes is Monday, August 25, through Sunday, August 31, 2025.

     If a certified assessor audits the books and records of a business which claims to be a
     “qualified business” for 2025 assessment purposes, one of the things the assessor should
     check is whether the business had 30 or fewer employees during the week of August 25,
     2025 through August 31, 2025.
