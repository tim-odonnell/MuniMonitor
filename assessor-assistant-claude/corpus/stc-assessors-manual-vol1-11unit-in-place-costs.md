                                                        CONVERSION INSTRUCTIONS
In order to convert the various Unit-in-Place costs into whole dollar costs, use the following formulas:
          UNIT-IN-PLACE                            BASE UNIT OF
                                                                                                CONVERSION FORMULA
            SECTION                                 MEASURE

As a final note, when determining costs for exterior walls and interior partitions, originally assume no window or door openings
to arrive at a total area for each unit. For example, an interior partition (wall) 8 feet high and 10 linear feet long with a
door opening would be measured as 80 square feet of interior partition.
