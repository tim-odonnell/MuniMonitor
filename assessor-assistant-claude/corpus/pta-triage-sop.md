# PTA / Uncapping Triage — Standard Operating Procedure (Statewide Default)

> **Statewide default SOP** — law-first practice drafted 2026-07-08 from MCL 211.27a/27b
> and the STC Transfer of Ownership Guidelines, designed to be adoptable by any Michigan
> assessing office. Items marked [LOCALIZE] are the points where offices differ; each
> states the default that applies until the adopting office overrides it. See
> `LOCALIZATION.md` for the full adoption checklist.
> Statutory requirements cited here are LAW and are restated for convenience only —
> the statute controls if this document ever disagrees with it.

## 1. Intake

Property Transfer Affidavits (Form 2766 / L-4260) arrive from transferees, who are
required by MCL 211.27a(10) to file within 45 days of a transfer. For every PTA (or
deed discovered without one), log at intake:

- Parcel number and property classification
- Transfer date and instrument type (warranty deed, quit claim, land contract,
  probate/estate document, trust instrument, other)
- Parties and any stated relationship between them
- Stated consideration, if any (NOTE: absence of a price does NOT mean no transfer
  of ownership — gifts and $1 conveyances can still uncap)
- Date the PTA was received (this drives the late-filing penalty math)

Entry goes in the office's CAMA system with the source document scanned and attached.
[LOCALIZE: name the CAMA system (BS&A, etc.) and the field/screen used for transfer
entries. Default until localized: record determinations in whatever notes field the
office uses consistently, one entry per transfer.]

## 2. Determination steps

1. **Is it a conveyance of a present interest?** Instruments solely securing a debt
   (mortgages) are not transfers of ownership. (MCL 211.27a(6))
2. **Does a statutory exception apply?** Check the claimed or apparent situation
   against the transfer-of-ownership exceptions in MCL 211.27a(7) and the STC
   Transfer of Ownership Guidelines — spouses/entireties, qualifying family transfers
   of residential real property (conditioned on use not changing), qualifying trust
   transfers (settlor/spouse present beneficiary), joint tenancy rules, and the other
   enumerated categories. **Match the facts to the specific statutory subsection —
   never approve an exception by general resemblance.**
3. **Family residential claims:** confirm the property is classified residential and
   that nothing indicates the use will change after transfer. If use is unknown,
   flag for follow-up — do not assume.
4. **Trust transfers:** identify settlor and present beneficiary from the trust
   instrument. If trust terms are not provided, request them in writing; the
   determination waits for the documents. Do not guess from the deed alone.
5. **No exception clearly applies →** mark as a transfer of ownership. Taxable value
   uncaps to SEV for the calendar year FOLLOWING the transfer (MCL 211.27a(3)).
6. **Routing:** staff complete steps 1-5 for clear cases. ANY claimed exception,
   trust, probate/estate matter, LLC or entity transfer, split/combination in the
   same season, or uncertainty of any kind routes to the assessor before entry.
   The assessor spot-checks a sample of routine staff determinations each month.
   [LOCALIZE: sample rate. Default: 1 in 10.]

## 3. Late-filing penalty (MCL 211.27b)

- Penalty applies when the PTA is filed (or the transfer is discovered) more than 45
  days after transfer: $5/day for real property not classified commercial/industrial,
  capped at $200 for an owner-occupied principal residence and $4,000 for other such
  property; $20/day capped at $1,000 for commercial/industrial (sale price ≤ $100M).
- The statute says the penalty **must** be levied. The only lawful waiver is a
  resolution of the governing body under MCL 211.27b(5).
- [LOCALIZE: whether the local governing body has adopted a 27b(5) waiver resolution,
  and its terms. **Default until localized: assume NO resolution exists — assess the
  penalty per statute; staff never waive case-by-case.** An adopting office should
  verify with its clerk/council records before changing this.]

## 4. Uncapping after failure to notify (MCL 211.27b mechanics)

When taxable value is adjusted because a transfer went unreported:
1. Certify additional taxes (and interest/penalty) for collection per 211.27b(2)-(3).
2. Notify the owner of the TV increase by first-class mail immediately; the notice
   starts a 35-day Michigan Tax Tribunal appeal window limited to whether a transfer
   occurred and arithmetic errors (211.27b(6)).
3. File the adjustment affidavit with the responsible officials within 30 days
   (211.27b(7)).

## 5. Missing-PTA monitoring (accuracy control)

Monthly: reconcile recorded instruments from the county register of deeds against
PTAs received. For each recorded transfer with no PTA on file, send the transferee a
follow-up letter with a blank L-4260 and a filing deadline. Track non-responses;
unresolved items get a determination from available evidence (deed, PRE filings,
utility/occupancy indicators) and route to the assessor.
[LOCALIZE: how recorded-deed information reaches the office (county register feed,
subscription service, manual pull) and the reconciliation day. Default: first business
week of each month, using whatever deed source the county makes available.]

## 6. Documentation standard (every determination, no exceptions)

A determination note in the CAMA record containing: instrument and transfer date;
determination (uncap effective YEAR / exempt under [specific subsection]); the key
facts relied on; the reference applied (statute subsection or STC guideline section);
date and initials of both the preparer and, where routed, the reviewing assessor.
Source documents (PTA, deed, trust excerpt, correspondence) scanned and attached.
Uncertain determinations are explicitly marked with the open questions listed.

## 7. Assistant usage note

The AI assistant may draft triage determinations and file notes under this SOP.
Every AI draft is reviewed by a human before entry; exception claims and anything
the assistant flags as uncertain follow the same routing to the assessor as staff
work under step 2.6.
