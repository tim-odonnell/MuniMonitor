GENERAL DESCRIPTION

Class CD Town Houses and Duplexes are constructed with minimum compliance to uniform building codes. The
building shape is usually rectangular, with little or no attention given to ornamentation or architectural design. The
overall quality of materials and workmanship is below average, and the interior is plain, with few refinements.
