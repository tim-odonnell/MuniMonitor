GENERAL DESCRIPTION

Class B Log Homes are above average in quality of both materials and workmanship. The floor plan may include
some custom interior features. Materials and workmanship are roughly equivalent to those of the Class B
conventional house. Substitute materials of equivalent price and quality may be found in place of some of the
materials listed below.
