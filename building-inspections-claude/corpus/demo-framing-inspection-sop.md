## Scheduling and prerequisites

Framing inspections are scheduled after rough electrical, plumbing, and mechanical
are complete and before insulation conceals the framing. The permit record and
approved plans must be on site [VERIFY department practice].

## On-site sequence (sketch)

1. Verify posted permit and approved plans match the work observed.
2. Walk the structure: joist/rafter layout against plans, hangers and connectors,
   notching and boring, headers and load paths, fire blocking, truss condition
   (any field-modified truss requires engineering documentation).
3. Record deficiencies with photographs; issue a correction notice listing each
   item with its code basis; re-inspection required after corrections.
4. Pass/fail is the registered inspector's determination, recorded in the permit
   system the same day.

## Photo screening note

Contractor-submitted or inspector photos may be pre-screened with the vision
assistant (`src/vision_check.py`) to draft a concern list before or after the site
visit. The screening output never substitutes for the on-site inspection.
