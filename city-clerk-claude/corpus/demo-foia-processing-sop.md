## Intake

Every FOIA request (email, mail, portal, counter) is logged the day received with:
date received, requester and contact, verbatim request text, and the calendared
statutory response date per the FOIA statute [VERIFY against MCL 15.235 as
ingested]. The FOIA coordinator is assigned all requests.

## Triage

1. Categorize the request and identify likely record locations/departments.
2. Screen for exemption-adjacent content (personnel, law enforcement,
   attorney-client, personal identifiers, security, litigation). ANY plausible
   exemption issue routes to the coordinator, and where legal judgment is
   involved, to the city attorney — before any records are gathered for release.
3. Overbroad or ambiguous requests get a clarification letter rather than a
   guess at scope.
4. Send the acknowledgment within the statutory window; extensions invoked per
   statute when needed, in writing.

## Assembly and review

Departments deliver responsive records to the coordinator, never directly to the
requester. The coordinator (with the attorney where flagged) makes all exemption,
redaction, and fee determinations. Redactions are logged with the exemption cited.

## The assistant's role

The AI assistant may draft triage screenings, acknowledgments, clarification
letters, and fee itemizations under this SOP. Every draft is reviewed by the
coordinator; the assistant never makes a disclosure decision.
